#### Macbee Modifications to Snapcast 0.35.0

All existing upstream copyright and license notices are retained verbatim. In accordance with GPLv3 Section 5(a), every modified Snapcast source file generated by [`tools/release_backend.py`](tools/release_backend.py) carries a prominent modification notice with a date near the top of the file. All Macbee modifications to Snapcast are licensed under `GPL-3.0-or-later`, without warranty:

- `client/player/file_player.hpp`, `client/player/file_player.cpp`: bounded synthetic null-sink metadata counters and Darwin playback-worker QoS promotion (`client-file`).
- `client/player/coreaudio_player.hpp`, `client/player/coreaudio_player.cpp`: CoreAudio metadata counters, startup priming-clock handling, `lastChunkTick` initialization, fail-closed runtime timestamp validation, diagnostic amplitude ceiling, and Darwin playback-worker QoS promotion (`client-coreaudio`).
- `full-range/coreaudio_player.cpp` (compiled as `snapclient-application`): full-range signed 16-bit playback variant lifting the diagnostic amplitude ceiling for ordinary audio playback.
- `server/streamreader/asio_stream.hpp`: opt-in V3 bounded pipe-clock telemetry, stale-media fail-closed guards, presentation-slot bridging, and bounded silence recovery for local `/dev/stdin` pipe streams.
- `server/snapserver.cpp`: Darwin playback-worker QoS promotion (`server-io`) on Asio server I/O threads.
- Sanitized offline isolated build recipe ([`tools/release_backend.py`](tools/release_backend.py)) with deterministic compiler/SDK provenance and build-path prefix mapping.
